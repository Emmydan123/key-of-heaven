QUESTIONS

Prayer Request FAQ

Can I submit a prayer request without being a member?

Yes. The prayer request form can be used by anyone who wants to share a request with the Key of Heaven prayer community.

Will my prayer request be public?

You can choose whether your request should remain with the prayer team or be shared with the prayer community.

Can I submit more than one request?

Yes. You can submit another request whenever you need prayer.

Can someone contact me about my request?

Yes. You can give permission in the form for a prayer-team member to contact you.
