KEY OF HEAVEN

OUR STORY

A Community Built Around Prayer

Key of Heaven is a prayer community created to bring people together in a shared journey of prayer, faith, encouragement and spiritual growth.

We believe that prayer is an important part of our relationship with Yahweh and that people can become stronger when they seek, pray and encourage one another together.

Key of Heaven provides a place where people can come, connect with the community, join prayer sessions, listen to previous prayers, submit prayer requests and learn more about our journey.

OUR VISION

A Community Rooted In Prayer

Our vision is to create a welcoming community where people can seek Yahweh, grow in faith, pray together and encourage one another.

OUR MISSION
