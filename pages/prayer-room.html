QUESTIONS

Prayer Room FAQ

Which days do we meet?

We meet for prayer on Monday and Friday, and we have our weekly Talk / Teaching session on Wednesday.

What time do we meet?

Our Monday, Wednesday and Friday sessions begin at 9:00.

What happens on Monday?

Monday is dedicated to prayer, thanksgiving and beginning the week together in faith.

What happens on Wednesday?

Wednesday is our Talk / Teaching session. It is a time to listen, learn, reflect and grow together.

What happens on Friday?

Friday is another prayer gathering where we come together for prayer, thanksgiving and fellowship.

Do I have to pray out loud?

No. You can listen and pray privately. You can participate aloud whenever you feel comfortable.

How can I join the online session?

You can join through the Google Meet link provided above. You can also join the official WhatsApp group to stay connected with the community.
